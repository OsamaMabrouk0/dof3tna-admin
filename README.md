## Overview

**Dof3tna Admin** is the control panel for the [Dof3tna](https://github.com/OsamaMabrouk0/dof3tna) student platform. It lets the owner and trusted content managers publish and maintain everything students see in the main app — lectures, summaries, schedules, announcements and more — directly from a phone.

## Who is it for?

| Role | Intended user |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | The platform owner. Full control over content, managers and app settings. |
| **Admin** | A content manager appointed by the owner, limited to the subjects and permissions assigned to them. |

If you are a student looking for the study app, you want the [**Dof3tna student app**](https://github.com/OsamaMabrouk0/dof3tna) instead.

## Getting access

1. **Contact the owner** (see [below](#contact-the-owner)) and tell them which subject or section you would like to help manage.
2. The owner creates your account and decides your **scope** (what you manage) and **permissions** (what you can do).
3. The owner gives you your sign-in details **personally**. Never share them with anyone.
4. Install the app, sign in, and change your password from **Settings**.

## Capabilities

What you can see and do depends on the permissions the owner assigned to you.

| | |
|---|---|
| 📚 **Content management** | Add, edit, hide and organize lectures, summaries and files per subject. |
| ✅ **Review & publish** | Review new files waiting for confirmation before they reach students. |
| 🔔 **Student notifications** | Send announcements and updates to students of a specific year or group. |
| 🗓️ **Schedules** | Publish and update lecture and section timetables. |
| 🎧 **Audio lectures** | Manage audio content. |
| 🏛️ **Buildings & rooms** | Maintain the campus buildings and rooms directory. |
| 🔗 **Batch links** | Manage group and drive links shown to students. |
| 📊 **Home dashboard** | An at-a-glance summary of activity and items that need attention. |
| 🧾 **Activity log** | A record of important actions taken in the app. |
| ⚙️ **App control** *(owner)* | Maintenance mode and publishing app updates. |
| 👥 **Managers** *(owner)* | Create, edit and deactivate admin accounts, and control their scope and permissions. |
| 🌗 **Light / Dark / Auto** | A full RTL interface that follows your device theme. |

## Roles & permissions

- The **owner** has access to everything.
- An **admin** only sees the sections the owner enabled for them, and only the subjects assigned to their scope.
- Owner-only screens (managers, activity log, app control, password requests) are not accessible to admins.
- The owner can change or revoke access at any time. Deactivated accounts are signed out.

## Forgot your password?

There is no e-mail reset. Instead:

1. On the sign-in screen, tap **Forgot password** and enter your account e-mail.
2. A request is sent to the owner.
3. The owner reviews it, approves it and gives you a **new password personally** (for example by phone call or chat).
4. Sign in with the new password and change it in **Settings**.

If you don't hear back, [contact the owner](#contact-the-owner) directly.

## Security & responsible use

- Your account is **personal**. Never share your credentials or let anyone use your session.
- Only publish content you are allowed to share. You are responsible for what you publish under your account.
- Sign out if you lose your phone and tell the owner immediately so access can be revoked.
- Actions in the app are logged.
- Found a security problem? Please **don't** post it publicly in an issue; message the owner privately instead.

<details>
<summary><b>Can I create my own account?</b></summary>

No. Accounts are created only by the owner. Please [contact the owner](#contact-the-owner) to request access.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>I installed the app but can't sign in.</b></summary>

Double-check your e-mail and password, and make sure the owner has activated your account. If it still fails, ask the owner to verify your account status.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>I can't see some sections.</b></summary>

Sections are shown according to the permissions the owner assigned. Ask the owner if you need more access.

<details>

This is normal for apps installed from outside a store. Allow the installation source when prompted, then tap **Install anyway**.
</details>

<details>

Download the new APK only from the official [Releases](https://github.com/OsamaMabrouk0/dof3tna-admin/releases) page. If it still fails, uninstall the old version and install the new one.
</details>

Not at the moment. The admin app is Android only.
</details>
